What is a Rising Desk?

A Rising desk also known as Standing Desk is a type of desk used for working while standing or sitting. According to researchers, Standing Desks/Rising Desk have many health benefits.


What are the health benefits of a Rising Desk?

  • It is more ergonomic as it provide a high-level of comfort by reducing strains on the body.
  • It creates a positive mindset on a working environment and encourage communication.
  • It increases productivity by enhancing creativity, memory(focus), and problem-solving capabilities.
  • It has been proved that standing for a long period of time can reduce the risk of cardiovascular diseases.
  • It has also been proved that standing can help the body to burn more calories.
